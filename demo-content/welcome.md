# Welcome to the Markdown Editor

A local-first place to write. Your files stay on your machine, in plain `.md` files you can move, sync, or open in any other tool whenever you want.

## What this is good for

* **Long-form writing.** Articles, essays, briefs, strategy docs.

* **Daily notes.** A single folder of dated markdown files becomes a writing log.

* **PRDs and one-pagers.** Drop a folder of project docs and edit them in place.

* **Anything you'd otherwise type into Notion** — without the lock-in.

## A few things to try

1. **Type something.** Auto-save kicks in 500 ms after you stop. The "Saved · 12:34" indicator at the bottom right confirms.
2. **Select a few words.** A floating toolbar appears with H2 / H3 / H4, bold, italic, link, and lists. Like Medium, but yours.
3. **Press** **`Cmd+/`** to flip into source view. The raw markdown lives there, with syntax highlighting.
4. **Press** **`Cmd+K`** to fuzzy-search files in the open folder.
5. **Drag a file** in the sidebar onto a folder to move it. Right-click for delete.

## Why local-first

> Cloud notes apps trade your control for their convenience. Local markdown trades back: you own the files, the format outlives any one tool, and you can grep, sync, version, or AI-assist them however you like.

If you've spent years writing into a tool that's now gating the export — this is the antidote.
